## Items
- Facial appearance :: a puffy (myxedematous) face, a malar rash or thickened lips, or their absence :: presence
- Hair / eyebrows :: dry, coarse hair, alopecia or loss of the outer third of the eyebrows, or their absence :: presence
- Skin texture :: dry, cold skin, xanthelasma or absent sweating, or their absence :: presence
- Eyes :: a cataract or its absence :: presence
- Mental / speech :: apathy or slurred, slow speech, or their absence :: presence
- Reflexes :: hyporeflexia with delayed relaxation, or its absence :: presence
- Peripheral neuropathy :: peripheral neuropathy or its absence :: presence
- Abdominal / bowel :: hepatomegaly or constipation, or their absence :: presence

## Related
- [[Thyroid]] :: Thyroid history :: a history of thyroid disease, thyroidectomy or radioiodine treatment, or its absence :: Myxedema is a manifestation of hypothyroidism, detailed in the thyroid history bundle
