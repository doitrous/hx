## Items
- Body habitus :: a moon face, plethoric face, truncal obesity, buffalo hump or a supraclavicular fat pad, or their absence :: presence
- Limbs / muscle :: thin limbs or muscle wasting, or their absence :: presence
- Skin :: thin skin, striae rubra, easy bruising, ulcers or acne, or their absence :: presence
- Androgenic signs :: hirsutism or acne, or their absence :: presence
- Pigmentation :: skin pigmentation or its absence :: presence
- Epigastric tenderness :: epigastric tenderness or its absence :: presence
- Visual field :: a visual field defect on confrontation testing, or its absence :: presence
- Edema :: lower limb edema or its absence :: presence
- Growth :: stunted growth in a child, or its absence :: presence
