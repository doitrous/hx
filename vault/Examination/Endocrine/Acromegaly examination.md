## Items
- Body build :: tall stature, a big skull, kyphosis or spade-like hands and feet :: text
- Voice :: a husky voice or its absence :: presence
- Skin sensation :: glove-and-stocking hypoesthesia, or its absence :: presence
- Nerve thickening :: a thickened ulnar nerve or other nerve, or its absence :: presence
- Cardiac / visceral enlargement :: cardiomegaly, hepatomegaly or other organomegaly, or their absence :: presence
- Breast changes :: gynecomastia or galactorrhea, or their absence :: presence
- Visual field :: a visual field defect on confrontation testing, or its absence :: presence
- Cranial nerves :: a third, fourth or sixth cranial nerve palsy, or its absence :: presence
