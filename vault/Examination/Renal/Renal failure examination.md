## Items
- Breathing pattern :: an abnormal breathing pattern, such as Kussmaul breathing, or its absence :: presence
- Pericardial rub :: a pericardial rub or its absence :: presence
- Chest findings :: a wheeze or a pleural rub, or their absence :: presence
- Uremic fetor / tongue :: uremic fetor or a dry, white coated tongue, or their absence :: presence
- Neurological signs :: apathy or a neurological deficit, or their absence :: presence
- Skin :: scratch marks or an earthy skin color, or their absence :: presence
- Edema :: ascites, lower limb or eyelid edema, or their absence :: presence
