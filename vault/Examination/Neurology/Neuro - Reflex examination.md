## Items
- Superficial reflexes :: superficial reflexes such as the abdominal or cremasteric reflex, or their absence :: text
- Plantar response :: the plantar (Babinski) response, or its absence :: presence
- Deep tendon reflexes :: deep tendon reflexes such as the biceps, triceps, knee or ankle jerk, or their absence :: text
- Reflex symmetry :: whether the reflexes are symmetrical, exaggerated or diminished :: text
- Clonus :: ankle, wrist or patellar clonus, or its absence :: presence
- Pathological reflexes :: a pathological reflex such as Hoffman's sign or a primitive reflex, or its absence :: presence
