## Items
- Variceal grade :: the grade or size of the varices, such as small or large, or grade I-III :: text
- Location :: the location of the varices, esophageal or gastric :: text
- Red signs :: red wale marks or other red signs on the varices, or their absence :: presence
- Number of bands placed :: the number of bands placed, or that none were placed :: number bands
- Injection agent and volume :: the sclerosant or glue agent used and its volume, or that injection was not performed :: text
- Active bleeding at treatment :: active bleeding seen at the time of treatment, or its absence :: presence
- Hemostasis achieved :: hemostasis achieved by the end of the procedure, or its absence :: presence
