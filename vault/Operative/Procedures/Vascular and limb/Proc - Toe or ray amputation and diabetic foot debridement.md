- Extent :: the extent of amputation or debridement, such as which toe, ray or tissue was removed :: text
- Tissue viability :: an assessment of tissue viability or bleeding at the margins, or its absence :: presence
- Bone sample :: a bone sample sent for culture or histology, or its absence :: presence
- Wound closure :: whether the wound was closed or left open :: presence
- Perfusion check :: a distal pulse or perfusion check before or after the procedure, or its absence :: presence
