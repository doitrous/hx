- Procedure type :: whether the procedure was an excision biopsy or a formal neck dissection :: text
- Levels dissected :: the neck levels dissected or nodes sampled :: text
- Nerves at risk :: identification or preservation of the accessory nerve, marginal mandibular nerve or other nerves at risk, or their absence :: presence
- Internal jugular vein :: preservation, ligation or reconstruction of the internal jugular vein, or its absence :: presence
- Frozen section :: use of frozen section, or that none was used :: presence
