## Items
- Thromboembolic symptoms :: a thromboembolic manifestation such as deep vein thrombosis or pulmonary embolism, or its absence :: presence
- Skin symptoms :: alopecia, oral or genital ulcers, rash, urticaria, pigment change or cold peripheries (Raynaud phenomenon), or their absence :: presence
- Eye symptoms :: red eye, dry eye, photophobia or eye pain, or their absence :: presence
- Lymph node symptoms :: enlarged lymph nodes, or their absence :: presence
- Hematological symptoms :: bleeding tendency or anemia, or their absence :: presence
- Muscle symptoms :: muscle pain or weakness, or their absence :: presence
