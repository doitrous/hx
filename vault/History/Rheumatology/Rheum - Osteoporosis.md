## Items
- Age at diagnosis :: age at diagnosis or how it was discovered :: text
- DEXA result :: a DEXA scan T-score, or that none is available :: number
- Fragility fracture :: a previous fragility fracture, or its absence :: presence
- Treatment :: a bisphosphonate or other osteoporosis treatment taken :: text
- Calcium / vitamin D :: calcium or vitamin D supplementation, or its absence :: presence
- Risk factors :: a documented risk factor such as menopause or long-term steroid use, or their absence :: presence
