## Items
- Postural symptoms :: postural dizziness, headache or postural hypotension, or their absence :: presence
- Hypoglycemic episodes :: blurred vision, confusion or an episode of coma from low blood sugar, or their absence :: presence
- Generalized weakness :: generalized weakness or its absence :: presence
- Weight loss :: weight loss or its absence :: presence
- Hyperpigmentation :: skin darkening of exposed areas, the axilla, the mouth or the areolae, or their absence :: presence
- Concentration / mentation :: difficulty concentrating, or its absence :: presence
- GI symptoms :: nausea, vomiting or gastric pain, or their absence :: presence
