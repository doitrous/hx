## Items
- Weight / BMI :: a current weight or BMI :: text
- Comorbidities :: an obesity-related comorbidity such as diabetes, hypertension or sleep apnea, or their absence :: presence
- Weight loss attempts :: previous diet, medication or weight loss attempts :: text
- Bariatric surgery :: a bariatric procedure, its type and date, or its absence :: presence
- Post-surgery complications :: a complication of bariatric surgery, or its absence :: presence
- Nutritional status :: a nutritional deficiency or supplementation after bariatric surgery, or their absence :: presence
