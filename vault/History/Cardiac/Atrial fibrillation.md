## Items
- Type / duration :: whether it is paroxysmal, persistent or permanent, and since when :: text
- Rate control :: rate-controlling medication such as a beta blocker or digoxin :: text
- Anticoagulation :: warfarin, a DOAC such as apixaban, rivaroxaban or dabigatran, or aspirin, or their absence :: presence
- INR / last check :: a recent INR value or anticoagulation check :: text
- Cardioversion / ablation :: a previous cardioversion or catheter ablation, or its absence :: presence
- Stroke / embolic history :: a previous stroke, TIA or other embolic event, or its absence :: presence
- Cardiology follow-up :: ongoing cardiology follow-up :: text

## Related
- [[Ischemic heart disease]] :: IHD screen :: a known diagnosis of ischemic heart disease, myocardial infarction or coronary intervention, or its absence :: Ischemic heart disease and atrial fibrillation commonly coexist
- [[Cardiac examination]] :: Cardiac exam :: a cardiac examination performed, or its absence :: Atrial fibrillation warrants a cardiac examination for rate, rhythm and murmurs
- [[Neuro - related symptoms]] :: Stroke screen :: weakness, speech disturbance or other neurological symptoms, or their absence :: Atrial fibrillation is a major risk factor for embolic stroke
- [[Valve disease]] :: Valve disease screen :: a known valve lesion or rheumatic heart disease, or its absence :: Mitral valve disease is a common cause of atrial fibrillation
