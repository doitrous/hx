## Items
- Valve and lesion :: which valve is affected and whether it is stenosis or regurgitation :: text
- Cause :: the cause, such as rheumatic fever, degenerative or congenital disease :: text
- Severity :: a recent echocardiogram severity grading :: text
- Prosthetic valve :: a prosthetic valve, and whether it is mechanical or bioprosthetic, or its absence :: presence
- Anticoagulation :: warfarin or another anticoagulant for a prosthetic valve, or its absence :: presence
- Endocarditis prophylaxis :: antibiotic prophylaxis advice for procedures, or its absence :: presence
- Previous infective endocarditis :: a previous episode of infective endocarditis, or its absence :: presence
- Symptoms :: exertional dyspnea, syncope or angina related to the valve disease :: text
