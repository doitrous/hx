## Items
- Lower urinary tract symptoms :: hesitancy, poor stream, frequency or nocturia, or their absence :: presence
- Retention :: a previous episode of urinary retention, or its absence :: presence
- Catheter :: a current or previous urinary catheter, or its absence :: presence
- PSA :: a PSA value, or that none is available :: number ng/mL
- Diagnosis :: the specific diagnosis, such as BPH, prostate cancer or bladder cancer :: text
- Treatment :: medical or surgical treatment received, such as TURP or prostatectomy :: text
- Hematuria :: hematuria or its absence :: presence
- Bilharzia exposure :: known schistosomiasis (bilharzia) exposure or treatment, or its absence :: presence
