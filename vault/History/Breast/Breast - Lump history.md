## Items
- Duration :: duration of the lump or complaint :: duration
- Change with cycle :: change in size or pain with the menstrual cycle :: text
- Nipple discharge :: nipple discharge, its character or its absence :: presence
- Skin change :: skin dimpling, retraction or color change over the breast, or their absence :: presence
- Risk factors :: family history of breast disease, age at menarche, parity or breastfeeding history :: text
- Constitutional :: weight loss, bone pain or other constitutional symptoms, or their absence :: presence
