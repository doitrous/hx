## Items
- Histology / receptor status :: the histology or hormone receptor (ER/PR/HER2) status :: text
- Staging :: a staging investigation or the stage of the cancer :: text
- Surgery :: a mastectomy, wide local excision (lumpectomy) or reconstruction, its date, or its absence :: presence
- Axillary surgery :: a sentinel node biopsy or axillary clearance, or its absence :: presence
- Neoadjuvant therapy :: neoadjuvant chemotherapy, or its absence :: presence
- Adjuvant therapy :: adjuvant chemotherapy, radiotherapy or hormonal therapy, or its absence :: presence
- Family history :: a family history of breast or ovarian cancer :: text
