## Items
- Smell disturbance :: loss of or change in the sense of smell, or its absence :: presence
- Visual symptoms :: reduced acuity, altered color vision or visual field loss, or their absence :: presence
- Ptosis or diplopia :: ptosis, squint or double vision, or their absence :: presence
- Facial sensory symptoms :: difficulty chewing, facial sensory change or numbness, or their absence :: presence
- Facial weakness :: drooling, mouth deviation, food accumulation in the cheek or loss of forehead wrinkling, or their absence :: presence
- Hearing / balance symptoms :: hearing loss, tinnitus or balance problems, or their absence :: presence
- Swallowing / voice symptoms :: difficulty swallowing, choking, difficulty speaking or hoarseness of voice, or their absence :: presence
- Shoulder / neck weakness :: dropped shoulder or neck weakness, or their absence :: presence
- Tongue deviation :: deviation of the tongue, or its absence :: presence
