## Items
- Date of event :: when the stroke or TIA occurred :: text
- Type :: whether it was ischemic or hemorrhagic, or a TIA :: text
- Deficit at the time :: the neurological deficit at the time, such as weakness, speech or facial involvement :: text
- Residual disability :: any residual weakness, speech or functional disability, or its absence :: presence
- Recurrence :: a further stroke or TIA since the first, or its absence :: presence
- Cause found :: atrial fibrillation, carotid disease or another cause found for the stroke :: text
