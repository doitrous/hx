## Items
- Distribution :: which limb or limbs are affected, and whether it is symmetrical :: text
- Cause :: a known cause, such as diabetes, alcohol, vitamin deficiency or an inherited condition :: text
- Onset pattern :: whether the onset was acute, as in Guillain-Barre syndrome, or gradual :: text
- Motor involvement :: weakness attributed to the neuropathy, or its absence :: presence
- Sensory involvement :: numbness or tingling attributed to the neuropathy, or its absence :: presence
- Respiratory involvement :: previous respiratory muscle weakness or ventilation for it, or its absence :: presence
- Residual disability :: residual weakness or disability, or its absence :: presence
