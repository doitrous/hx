## Items
- Distribution :: whether the enlarged nodes are localized or generalized, and which regions are involved :: text
- Neck pressure symptoms :: hoarseness, dyspnea, dysphagia, facial edema or Horner syndrome, or their absence :: presence
- Chest symptoms :: chest pain, dyspnea or cough, or their absence :: presence
- Axillary compression :: arm edema, ischemia, gangrene, tingling or numbness, or their absence :: presence
- Abdominal / retroperitoneal symptoms :: abdominal pain, back pain, jaundice, leg edema or renal pain, or their absence :: presence
- Inguinal / lower limb compression :: leg edema, ischemia, gangrene, varicose veins or claudication, or their absence :: presence
- Fever pattern :: fever, including a hectic, night-predominant or Pel-Ebstein pattern, or its absence :: presence
- Rash :: a rash associated with the lymph node swelling, or its absence :: presence
