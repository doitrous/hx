## Items
- Claudication distance :: the claudication distance :: text
- Rest pain :: rest pain, or its absence :: presence
- Color change :: color change in the limb, such as pallor or cyanosis, or its absence :: presence
- Coldness :: coldness of the limb, or its absence :: presence
- Risk factors :: smoking, diabetes or hyperlipidemia :: text
- Previous vascular intervention :: previous vascular surgery or intervention, or its absence :: presence
