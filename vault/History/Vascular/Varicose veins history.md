## Items
- Duration :: duration of the varicose veins :: duration
- Pregnancy :: number of pregnancies, or their absence :: text
- Previous DVT :: a previous deep vein thrombosis, or its absence :: presence
- Occupation :: an occupation involving prolonged standing :: text
- Complications :: leg ulcer, bleeding, eczema or thrombophlebitis, or their absence :: presence
- Family history :: a family history of varicose veins, or its absence :: presence
