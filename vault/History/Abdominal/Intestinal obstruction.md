## Items
- Pain :: colicky abdominal pain, or its absence :: presence
- Vomiting :: vomiting, its content or frequency :: text
- Distension :: abdominal distension, or its absence :: presence
- Absolute constipation :: absolute constipation (no passage of flatus or stool), or its absence :: presence
- Previous abdominal surgery :: previous abdominal surgery or known adhesions, or their absence :: presence
- Imaging :: an X-ray or CT finding of obstruction, such as dilated bowel loops or air-fluid levels :: text
- Management :: conservative management (NG tube, drip and suck) or surgery for it :: text
