## Items
- Site :: the site of the hernia, such as inguinal, femoral, umbilical or incisional :: text
- Duration :: duration of the swelling or hernia :: duration
- Reducibility :: whether it is reducible, or its absence :: presence
- Precipitating factors :: chronic cough, constipation, straining at micturition or heavy lifting, or their absence :: presence
- Previous repair :: a previous hernia repair, its date, approach and any mesh used, or its absence :: presence
- Recurrence :: recurrence after previous repair, or its absence :: presence
