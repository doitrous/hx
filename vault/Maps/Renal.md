# Renal

Map of content for the Renal bundles.

## History

- [[Renal - Chronic renal failure]]
    - → [[Hypertension]]: Hypertension is both a cause and a complication of chronic kidney disease
    - → [[Diabetes mellitus]]: Diabetic nephropathy is a leading cause of chronic kidney disease
    - → [[Edema - limb swelling]]: Fluid retention in renal failure causes edema, detailed in the edema bundle
    - → [[Heme - Bleeding tendency]]: Uremia impairs platelet function and causes a bleeding tendency
    - → [[Renal failure examination]]: Chronic renal failure warrants examination for uremic signs, edema and pericardial rub
    - → [[Heme - Blood disorder]]: Chronic kidney disease causes anemia from reduced erythropoietin production
    - → [[Cardiac examination]]: Chronic renal failure causes fluid overload and uremic pericarditis warranting cardiac examination
- [[Renal - Dialysis and renal transplant]]
    - → [[Renal - Chronic renal failure]]: Dialysis and transplantation are treatments for end-stage chronic kidney disease
    - → [[Heme - Blood disorder]]: Renal failure and dialysis cause anemia from reduced erythropoietin
    - → [[Hypertension]]: Hypertension commonly accompanies end-stage renal disease
- [[Renal - Prostate and lower urinary tract disease]]
    - → [[Urinary symptoms]]: Prostate and bladder disease are analyzed further in the urinary symptoms bundle
    - → [[Renal - Chronic renal failure]]: Chronic bladder outlet obstruction can cause chronic kidney disease
- [[Renal - Urinary stone disease]]
    - → [[Urinary symptoms]]: Active stone disease is analyzed further in the urinary symptoms bundle
    - → [[Renal - Chronic renal failure]]: Recurrent or obstructing stones can cause chronic kidney disease
- [[Urinary symptoms]]
    - → [[Renal - Chronic renal failure]]: Urinary symptoms combined with reduced output can indicate underlying chronic kidney disease

    - → [[Hematology examination]]: Chronic renal failure causes anemia and a bleeding tendency
