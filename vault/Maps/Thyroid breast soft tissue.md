# Thyroid breast soft tissue (Operative)

Map of content for Thyroid breast soft tissue procedures.

## Procedures

- [[Proc - Excision of lump cyst or lipoma]]
    - → [[Op - Specimen]]: The decision to send or discard the specimen must be documented
- [[Proc - Mastectomy]]
    - → [[Op - Drain]]: Mastectomy routinely documents drain use to manage the dead space created
    - → [[Op - Specimen]]: Orientation is needed to localise margins on the specimen
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: Nerves crossing the operative field are at risk during mastectomy
- [[Proc - Neck lymph node biopsy and dissection]]
    - → [[Op - Drain]]: Neck dissections routinely document drain use to prevent haematoma or seroma
    - → [[Op - Specimen]]: The nodes and levels must be tracked correctly for histology
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: Several named nerves pass through the neck levels dissected
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: The neck is highly vascular and dissection carries a bleeding risk
- [[Proc - Parathyroidectomy]]
    - → [[Op - Specimen]]: The gland(s) must be tracked and correctly identified for histology
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: The nerve lies close to the parathyroid glands and is at risk during dissection
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: The neck is highly vascular and dissection carries a bleeding risk
- [[Proc - Parotidectomy]]
    - → [[Op - Drain]]: Parotidectomy routinely documents drain use to prevent haematoma
    - → [[Op - Specimen]]: The specimen must be tracked correctly for histology
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: The facial nerve runs through the gland and is the principal structure at risk
- [[Proc - Sentinel node biopsy and axillary clearance]]
    - → [[Op - Drain]]: Axillary clearance routinely documents drain use to reduce seroma
    - → [[Op - Specimen]]: Node counts and levels must be tracked correctly for staging
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: Several named nerves cross the axilla and are at risk during clearance
- [[Proc - Skin graft]]
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: Both donor and recipient sites carry a bleeding risk
- [[Proc - Skin lesion excision]]
    - → [[Op - Specimen]]: The orientation marker must be matched to the specimen for margin reporting
- [[Proc - Thyroidectomy total and hemi]]
    - → [[Op - Drain]]: A thyroid bed haematoma can compress the airway, so drain use is routinely documented
    - → [[Op - Specimen]]: The specimen must be tracked for histology and identification of lobes
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: These nerves are the principal structures at risk in thyroid surgery
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: Thyroid bed bleeding can rapidly compress the airway and is a recognized complication
- [[Proc - Wide local excision or lumpectomy]]
    - → [[Op - Specimen]]: Correct labelling is needed to match margins to the specimen orientation
- [[Proc - Surgical debridement of a wound]]
    - → [[Op - Contamination and washout]]: Debridement of contaminated or necrotic tissue is usually accompanied by washout
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: Debridement of vascular tissue carries a bleeding risk
