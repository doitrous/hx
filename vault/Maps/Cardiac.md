# Cardiac

Map of content for the Cardiac bundles.

## History

- [[Atrial fibrillation]]
    - → [[Ischemic heart disease]]: Ischemic heart disease and atrial fibrillation commonly coexist
    - → [[Cardiac examination]]: Atrial fibrillation warrants a cardiac examination for rate, rhythm and murmurs
    - → [[Neuro - related symptoms]]: Atrial fibrillation is a major risk factor for embolic stroke
    - → [[Valve disease]]: Mitral valve disease is a common cause of atrial fibrillation
- [[Cardiac - related symptoms]]
    - → [[Palpitation]]: A positive screen opens the palpitation bundle for onset, rate and rhythm
    - → [[Dyspnea]]: A positive screen opens the dyspnea bundle for grade, orthopnea and relieving factors
    - → [[Edema - limb swelling]]: A positive screen opens the edema bundle for laterality, level and pitting
    - → [[Cardiac examination]]: A cardiac complaint warrants the full cardiac examination for heart sounds and murmurs
    - → [[Fever]]: A positive screen opens the fever bundle for onset, course and associated rigors
- [[Pacemaker or ICD]]
    - → [[Ischemic heart disease]]: Ischemic heart disease is a common indication for a cardiac device
    - → [[Atrial fibrillation]]: Some devices are implanted for arrhythmia or heart block
    - → [[Cardiac examination]]: A cardiac device warrants a cardiac examination
    - → [[Heart failure]]: CRT devices are implanted for heart failure with dyssynchrony
- [[Edema - limb swelling]]
    - → [[Jaundice]]: Liver disease with hypoalbuminemia is a differential cause of limb edema
- [[Heart failure]]
    - → [[Ischemic heart disease]]: Ischemic heart disease is a leading cause of heart failure
    - → [[Edema - limb swelling]]: Heart failure commonly causes limb edema
    - → [[Cardiac examination]]: Heart failure warrants the full cardiac examination
    - → [[Hypertension]]: Hypertension is a common cause of heart failure
- [[Hypertension]]
    - → [[Renal - Chronic renal failure]]: Hypertension is both a cause and consequence of chronic kidney disease
    - → [[Vascular - Limb ischemia history]]: Hypertension is a major risk factor for peripheral arterial disease
    - → [[Cardiac examination]]: Hypertension is a major risk factor for hypertensive heart disease and warrants cardiac examination
    - → [[Neuro - related symptoms]]: Hypertension is a major risk factor for stroke
- [[Ischemic heart disease]]
    - → [[Hypertension]]: Hypertension is a major shared risk factor for ischemic heart disease
    - → [[Diabetes mellitus]]: Diabetes accelerates coronary artery disease
    - → [[Cardiac examination]]: Ischemic heart disease warrants the full cardiac examination
    - → [[Heart failure]]: Ischemic heart disease is a leading cause of heart failure
- [[Palpitation]]
    - → [[Cardiac examination]]: Palpitation warrants a cardiac examination for rate, rhythm and murmurs
- [[Valve disease]]
    - → [[Cardiac examination]]: Valve disease warrants the full cardiac examination for murmurs
    - → [[Heart failure]]: Valve disease is a common cause of heart failure
    - → [[Atrial fibrillation]]: Mitral valve disease commonly causes atrial fibrillation
    - → [[Dyspnea]]: Valve disease commonly presents with exertional dyspnea
