# Obstetrics and Gynecology (Operative)

Map of content for Obstetrics and Gynecology procedures.

## Procedures

- [[Proc - Caesarean section]]
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: Postpartum hemorrhage is a recognised complication of caesarean section
    - → [[Op - Blood transfusion]]: Significant obstetric bleeding may require transfusion
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: Adhesions from a prior caesarean increase the risk of visceral injury
- [[Proc - Hysterectomy]]
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: The uterine vessels carry a significant bleeding risk during hysterectomy
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: These structures lie close to the uterus and are at risk during hysterectomy
    - → [[Op - Laparoscopic access]]: A laparoscopic hysterectomy depends on safe abdominal access
    - → [[Op - Conversion to open]]: A laparoscopic or vaginal hysterectomy may need to be converted to open for bleeding or difficult anatomy
