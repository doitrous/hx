# Operative events

Map of content for intraoperative event bundles (fire during any procedure).

## Events

- [[Op - Difficult or abandoned procedure]]
- [[Op - Adhesiolysis]]
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: Adhesiolysis carries a recognised risk of bowel or vascular injury requiring repair
- [[Op - Cardiorespiratory event]]
- [[Op - Resection and anastomosis]]
    - → [[Op - Drain]]: A drain is commonly left next to a bowel anastomosis to monitor for leak or bleeding
    - → [[Op - Stoma formation]]: A covering stoma is used to protect a high-risk anastomosis
    - → [[Op - Contamination and washout]]: Resection of bowel commonly spills contaminated content requiring washout
    - → [[Op - Specimen]]: The resected segment is itself a specimen requiring orientation and labelling
- [[Op - Bleeding]]
    - → [[Op - Blood transfusion]]: Significant bleeding is the usual reason a transfusion is required
    - → [[Op - Drain]]: A drain is often left to monitor ongoing bleeding after a bleeding event

- [[Op - Fixation hardware]]
    - → [[Op - Intraoperative imaging]]: Fixation hardware position is routinely confirmed with intraoperative imaging
    - → [[Op - Tourniquet]]: Limb fixation procedures commonly use a tourniquet for a bloodless field

- [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]
    - → [[Op - Conversion to open]]: A significant iatrogenic injury may require conversion to repair safely
    - → [[Nerve deformity and wasting examination]]: A recognized nerve injury should prompt a focused examination for wasting and deformity
- [[Op - Laparoscopic access]]
    - → [[Op - Conversion to open]]: Difficulty with laparoscopic access is a common reason for conversion
