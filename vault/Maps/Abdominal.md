# Abdominal

Map of content for the Abdominal bundles.

## History

- [[Abdominal - Ascites and swelling]]
    - → [[Swelling - lump]]: A localized abdominal swelling is analyzed using the same characteristics as any other lump
- [[Abdominal - Constitutional symptoms]]
    - → [[Fever]]: The fever is analyzed in detail in its own bundle
    - → [[Heme - Bleeding tendency]]: The sheet lists a bleeding tendency alongside toxic and malignant constitutional features and with splenomegaly
    - → [[Lymph - Lymphadenopathy]]: The sheet lists lymphadenopathy alongside splenomegaly in the constitutional workup
    - → [[Heme - Blood disorder]]: The constitutional workup for chronic disease includes screening for an underlying blood disorder such as anemia
- [[Abdominal - related symptoms]]
    - → [[Vomiting]]: A positive screen opens the vomiting bundle for onset, content and relieving factors
    - → [[Hematemesis]]: A positive screen opens the hematemesis bundle for amount, color and associated bleeding
    - → [[Jaundice]]: A positive screen opens the jaundice bundle for stool and urine color and pruritus
    - → [[Bowel symptoms]]: A positive screen opens the bowel symptom bundle for onset, consistency and blood or mucus
    - → [[Abdominal examination]]: An abdominal complaint warrants the full abdominal examination for tenderness, organomegaly and bowel sounds
    - → [[Fever]]: A positive screen opens the fever bundle for onset, course and associated rigors
- [[Acute appendicitis]]
    - → [[Vomiting]]: Vomiting commonly accompanies acute appendicitis
    - → [[Abdominal - Constitutional symptoms]]: Acute appendicitis is a common toxic abdominal condition
    - → [[Abdominal examination]]: Appendicitis warrants examination for right iliac fossa tenderness and peritonism
- [[Bowel symptoms]]
    - → [[General surgery - local effects]]: A change in bowel habit with bleeding raises suspicion for colorectal malignancy requiring assessment of local and metastatic effects
    - → [[Heme - Bleeding tendency]]: Bleeding per rectum may be one manifestation of a systemic bleeding tendency
- [[Gallstone disease]]
    - → [[Jaundice]]: Obstructive jaundice is a common complication of CBD stones
    - → [[Fever]]: Cholangitis presents with fever as part of Charcot's triad
    - → [[Pancreatitis]]: Gallstones are a leading cause of acute pancreatitis
    - → [[Abdominal examination]]: Biliary disease warrants examination for right upper quadrant tenderness
- [[GI malignancy]]
    - → [[Bowel symptoms]]: Colorectal cancer commonly presents with a change in bowel habit
    - → [[Abdominal - Constitutional symptoms]]: GI malignancy commonly causes constitutional symptoms
    - → [[Family history]]: Colorectal cancer risk is affected by family history
    - → [[General surgery - local effects]]: A GI malignancy needs its local and metastatic effects assessed
- [[Hematemesis]]
    - → [[Jaundice]]: Variceal or gastritis bleeding from liver disease often coexists with jaundice
    - → [[Heme - Bleeding tendency]]: Hematemesis may be one manifestation of a systemic bleeding tendency
- [[Inflammatory bowel disease]]
    - → [[Bowel symptoms]]: IBD flares are analyzed with the general bowel symptom bundle
    - → [[Perianal disease]]: Crohn's disease commonly causes perianal fistulizing disease
    - → [[Abdominal - Constitutional symptoms]]: IBD flares commonly cause constitutional symptoms
- [[Jaundice]]
    - → [[Abdominal - Ascites and swelling]]: Liver disease presenting with jaundice commonly causes ascites from portal hypertension
- [[Liver cirrhosis and chronic hepatitis]]
    - → [[Hematemesis]]: Portal hypertension from cirrhosis causes variceal bleeding
    - → [[Jaundice]]: Hepatocellular decompensation commonly presents with jaundice
    - → [[Abdominal - Ascites and swelling]]: Portal hypertension is a leading cause of ascites
    - → [[Heme - Bleeding tendency]]: Impaired synthetic liver function causes coagulopathy and a bleeding tendency
- [[Intestinal obstruction]]
    - → [[Vomiting]]: Vomiting is a cardinal feature of intestinal obstruction, analyzed in its own bundle
    - → [[Hernia examination]]: An obstructed or strangulated hernia is a common cause of intestinal obstruction
    - → [[Bowel symptoms]]: Obstruction from a colonic mass may present with an antecedent change in bowel habit
    - → [[Abdominal examination]]: Obstruction warrants examination for distension, visible peristalsis and bowel sounds
- [[Pancreatitis]]
    - → [[Vomiting]]: Vomiting commonly accompanies acute pancreatitis
    - → [[Gallstone disease]]: Gallstones are the leading cause of acute pancreatitis
    - → [[Diabetes mellitus]]: Chronic pancreatitis causes secondary diabetes from endocrine insufficiency
    - → [[Abdominal examination]]: Pancreatitis warrants examination for epigastric tenderness and signs of severity
- [[Perianal disease]]
    - → [[Bowel symptoms]]: Perianal bleeding is assessed alongside the general bowel symptom bundle
    - → [[Ulcer history]]: A pilonidal sinus or fistula tract behaves as a chronic non-healing wound
- [[Peptic ulcer disease]]
    - → [[Hematemesis]]: A bleeding peptic ulcer is a common cause of hematemesis
    - → [[Bowel symptoms]]: A bleeding peptic ulcer commonly presents with melena
    - → [[Abdominal examination]]: Peptic ulcer disease warrants examination for epigastric tenderness or peritonism
- [[Vomiting]]
    - → [[Abdominal - Ascites and swelling]]: Vomiting from bowel obstruction is commonly associated with abdominal distension
