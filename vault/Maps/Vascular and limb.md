# Vascular and limb (Operative)

Map of content for Vascular and limb procedures.

## Procedures

- [[Proc - AV fistula creation]]
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: Vascular anastomosis for a fistula carries a bleeding risk
- [[Proc - Below knee and above knee amputation]]
    - → [[Op - Tourniquet]]: A tourniquet may be used to control blood loss during a limb amputation
    - → [[Op - Specimen]]: The amputated specimen's disposal or histology referral must be tracked
    - → [[Op - Contamination and washout]]: Amputation for infection or ischaemia often involves contaminated tissue requiring washout
    - → [[Op - Blood transfusion]]: Major limb amputation carries a significant risk of blood loss requiring transfusion
- [[Proc - Endovenous ablation]]
    - → [[Op - Conversion to open]]: Endovenous ablation may need to convert to open surgery if the vein cannot be cannulated or ablated
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: The saphenous or sural nerve runs close to the treated vein and is at risk during ablation
- [[Proc - Femoral embolectomy]]
    - → [[Proc - Fasciotomy]]: Reperfusion after embolectomy can cause compartment syndrome requiring fasciotomy
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: Arteriotomy closure carries a bleeding risk
- [[Proc - Toe or ray amputation and diabetic foot debridement]]
    - → [[Op - Specimen]]: The excised tissue and any bone sample must be tracked for laboratory analysis
    - → [[Op - Contamination and washout]]: Diabetic foot debridement is usually for infected or contaminated tissue
- [[Proc - Varicose vein surgery]]
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: The saphenous or sural nerve lies close to the vein being stripped and is at risk of injury
