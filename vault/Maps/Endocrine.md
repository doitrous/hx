# Endocrine

Map of content for the Endocrine bundles.

## History

- [[Diabetes mellitus]]
    - → [[Renal - Chronic renal failure]]: Diabetic nephropathy is a leading cause of chronic kidney disease
    - → [[Ulcer history]]: Diabetic neuropathy and ischemia predispose to non-healing foot ulcers
    - → [[Vascular - Limb ischemia history]]: Diabetes accelerates peripheral arterial disease and limb ischemia
    - → [[Neuro - Sensory symptoms]]: Diabetic peripheral neuropathy commonly causes distal sensory symptoms
- [[Endo - Acromegaly]]
    - → [[Diabetes mellitus]]: Growth hormone excess in acromegaly commonly causes diabetes mellitus
    - → [[Acromegaly examination]]: A positive screen for acromegaly warrants the acromegaly examination for confirmatory signs
- [[Endo - Addison disease]]
    - → [[Adrenal disease examination]]: A positive screen for Addison disease warrants the adrenal disease examination
- [[Endo - Cushing syndrome]]
    - → [[Hypertension]]: Cortisol excess in Cushing syndrome commonly causes hypertension
    - → [[Diabetes mellitus]]: Cortisol excess in Cushing syndrome commonly causes diabetes mellitus
    - → [[Edema - limb swelling]]: Cortisol has mineralocorticoid activity and causes fluid retention
    - → [[Adrenal disease examination]]: A positive screen for Cushing syndrome warrants the adrenal disease examination
- [[Obesity and bariatric history]]
    - → [[Diabetes mellitus]]: Obesity is a major risk factor for type 2 diabetes and bariatric surgery often improves it
    - → [[Hypertension]]: Obesity is a major risk factor for hypertension
- [[Thyroid]]
    - → [[Thyroid examination]]: A thyroid history warrants the full thyroid examination for size, consistency and toxic signs
    - → [[Myxedema examination]]: Hypothyroid features on history warrant the myxedema examination
    - → [[Cardiac examination]]: Thyrotoxicosis can cause tachyarrhythmia and warrants cardiac examination
- [[Thyroid - Neck swelling]]
    - → [[Swelling - lump]]: A thyroid swelling is analyzed using the same characteristics as any other lump
    - → [[Pain]]: The sheet's thyroid pain section maps to the general pain analysis

## Examination

- [[Acromegaly examination]]
    - → [[Diabetes mellitus]]: Growth hormone excess in acromegaly commonly causes diabetes mellitus
- [[Adrenal disease examination]]
    - → [[Hypertension]]: Adrenal disease affects blood pressure in both directions
    - → [[Diabetes mellitus]]: Cortisol or catecholamine excess in adrenal disease commonly causes diabetes mellitus
- [[Myxedema examination]]
    - → [[Thyroid]]: Myxedema is a manifestation of hypothyroidism, detailed in the thyroid history bundle
