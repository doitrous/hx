# Orthopedics (Operative)

Map of content for Orthopedics procedures.

## Procedures

- [[Proc - ACL reconstruction]]
    - → [[Op - Tourniquet]]: A positive screen opens the tourniquet event bundle for site, pressure and duration
- [[Proc - Carpal tunnel release]]
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: These structures lie directly beneath the ligament being divided
    - → [[Op - Tourniquet]]: A positive screen opens the tourniquet event bundle for site, pressure and duration
- [[Proc - Closed reduction and casting]]
    - → [[Op - Intraoperative imaging]]: Post-reduction imaging is needed to confirm the fracture or joint position
- [[Proc - Dynamic hip screw]]
    - → [[Op - Fixation hardware]]: The implanted device should be traceable for future imaging and revision
    - → [[Op - Intraoperative imaging]]: Fluoroscopy is used to confirm guidewire and screw position
    - → [[Op - Blood transfusion]]: Hip fracture surgery in this population often requires transfusion
- [[Proc - External fixator application]]
    - → [[Op - Fixation hardware]]: The frame and pins are implanted hardware that should be traceable for removal
    - → [[Op - Intraoperative imaging]]: Fluoroscopy is used to confirm pin placement and reduction
- [[Proc - Fasciotomy]]
    - → [[Op - Contamination and washout]]: Fasciotomy wounds are often revisited for washout and delayed closure
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: Fasciotomy wounds are highly vascular and bleeding is common
- [[Proc - Hip hemiarthroplasty and total hip replacement]]
    - → [[Op - Mesh or implant]]: Implanted prosthesis details should be traceable for future imaging and revision
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: Hip arthroplasty carries a significant risk of blood loss requiring control
    - → [[Op - Blood transfusion]]: Hip fracture surgery in this population often requires transfusion
- [[Proc - Intramedullary nailing]]
    - → [[Op - Fixation hardware]]: The implanted nail should be traceable for future imaging and revision
    - → [[Op - Intraoperative imaging]]: Fluoroscopy is used throughout nailing to confirm entry, reduction and locking
    - → [[Op - Tourniquet]]: A limb tourniquet may be used during nailing of the tibia or forearm
    - → [[Op - Cardiorespiratory event]]: Reaming and nailing of long bones carries a risk of fat embolism syndrome and cardiorespiratory compromise
- [[Proc - Knee arthroscopy]]
    - → [[Op - Conversion to open]]: Arthroscopic findings can require conversion to an open procedure
    - → [[Op - Tourniquet]]: A positive screen opens the tourniquet event bundle for site, pressure and duration
- [[Proc - K-wire fixation]]
    - → [[Op - Fixation hardware]]: The wires are implanted hardware that should be traceable for removal or revision
    - → [[Op - Intraoperative imaging]]: Fluoroscopy is used to confirm wire position and reduction
    - → [[Op - Tourniquet]]: A limb tourniquet is often used for a bloodless field during K-wire fixation of hand or foot fractures
- [[Proc - Laminectomy or discectomy]]
    - → [[Op - Iatrogenic injury]]: The nerve roots and dura lie directly in the operative field
    - → [[Op - Intraoperative imaging]]: Level localisation is confirmed with intraoperative imaging
- [[Proc - ORIF general]]
    - → [[Op - Fixation hardware]]: Implanted hardware should be traceable for future imaging and revision
    - → [[Op - Intraoperative imaging]]: Imaging is used to confirm reduction and implant position
    - → [[Op - Tourniquet]]: A limb tourniquet is often used to reduce blood loss during ORIF
    - → [[Op - Cardiorespiratory event]]: Long bone fixation carries a risk of fat embolism syndrome and cardiorespiratory compromise
- [[Proc - Shoulder arthroscopy]]
    - → [[Op - Conversion to open]]: Arthroscopic findings can require conversion to an open repair
- [[Proc - Tendon repair]]
    - → [[Op - Tourniquet]]: A positive screen opens the tourniquet event bundle for site, pressure and duration
- [[Proc - Total knee replacement]]
    - → [[Op - Mesh or implant]]: Implanted prosthesis details should be traceable for future imaging and revision
    - → [[Op - Tourniquet]]: A positive screen opens the tourniquet event bundle for site, pressure and duration
    - → [[Op - Blood transfusion]]: Total knee replacement carries a moderate risk of blood loss requiring transfusion
