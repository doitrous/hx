# Hematology

Map of content for the Hematology bundles.

## History

- [[Heme - Anemia workup]]
    - → [[Heme - Blood disorder]]: Anemia is analyzed alongside other blood disorder symptoms in that bundle
    - → [[Bowel symptoms]]: Chronic gastrointestinal blood loss is a common cause of iron deficiency anemia
- [[Heme - Anticoagulant or antiplatelet therapy]]
    - → [[Heme - Bleeding tendency]]: Antithrombotic therapy is a common cause of an acquired bleeding tendency
    - → [[Neuro - Stroke or TIA]]: Secondary stroke prevention is a common indication for antithrombotic therapy
    - → [[Hypertension]]: Cardiovascular risk factors commonly coexist with the indication for antithrombotic therapy
- [[Heme - Bleeding tendency]]
    - → [[Hematemesis]]: Hematemesis is one of the bleeding orifices on the sheet and is analyzed in its own bundle
    - → [[Bowel symptoms]]: Bleeding per rectum is one of the bleeding orifices on the sheet and is analyzed in the bowel symptoms bundle
    - → [[Hematology examination]]: A bleeding tendency warrants the hematology examination for purpura and mucosal bleeding signs
- [[Heme - Blood disorder]]
    - → [[Heme - Bleeding tendency]]: A bleeding tendency is analyzed in detail in its own bundle
    - → [[Jaundice]]: Hemolysis presents with jaundice, detailed in the Jaundice bundle
    - → [[Hematology examination]]: A blood disorder history warrants the hematology examination for pallor, purpura and organomegaly
- [[Heme - Inherited blood disorder]]
    - → [[Heme - Blood disorder]]: Inherited blood disorders are analyzed alongside general blood disorder symptoms in that bundle
    - → [[Heme - Bleeding tendency]]: Hemophilia and von Willebrand disease present with a bleeding tendency detailed in that bundle
- [[Heme - Malignancy and treatment]]
    - → [[Heme - Blood disorder]]: Hematological malignancy can infiltrate the marrow and other organs, detailed in that bundle
    - → [[Lymph - Lymphadenopathy]]: Lymphoma and leukemia commonly present with lymphadenopathy
    - → [[Abdominal - Constitutional symptoms]]: B symptoms are a key feature of hematological malignancy staging
