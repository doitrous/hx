# Endoscopy (Operative)

Map of content for Endoscopy procedures.

## Procedures

- [[Proc - Colonoscopy]]
    - → [[Proc - Polypectomy]]: Colonoscopy commonly includes polyp removal, which has its own documentation requirements

- [[Proc - Polypectomy]]
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: Post-polypectomy bleeding is the most common complication of colonoscopic polypectomy
- [[Proc - Upper GI bleed therapeutic endoscopy]]
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: This item captures the clinical bleeding picture behind the therapeutic endoscopy already known to have been performed
    - → [[Op - Blood transfusion]]: Upper GI hemorrhage often requires transfusion
- [[Proc - Upper GI endoscopy]]
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: Biopsy during upper GI endoscopy carries a small bleeding risk
- [[Proc - Variceal management]]
    - → [[Op - Bleeding]]: Variceal treatment is frequently performed for active or recent hemorrhage
    - → [[Op - Blood transfusion]]: Variceal hemorrhage often requires transfusion
