# Chest

Map of content for the Chest bundles.

## History

- [[Asthma]]
    - → [[Cough]]: Asthma commonly presents with cough
    - → [[Dyspnea]]: Asthma commonly presents with breathlessness
    - → [[Chest examination]]: Asthma warrants a chest examination for wheeze and air entry
- [[Chest - related symptoms]]
    - → [[Cough]]: A positive screen opens the cough bundle for onset, character and diurnal variation
    - → [[Expectoration]]: A positive screen opens the sputum bundle for amount, color and consistency
    - → [[Hemoptysis]]: A positive screen opens the hemoptysis bundle for amount, color and number of attacks
    - → [[Dyspnea]]: A positive screen opens the dyspnea bundle for onset, orthopnea and exertional grade
    - → [[Chest examination]]: A respiratory complaint warrants the full chest examination for percussion and breath sounds
    - → [[Abdominal - Constitutional symptoms]]: A positive screen opens the shared constitutional symptom bundle used across systems for a systemic or malignant cause
- [[COPD]]
    - → [[Cough]]: COPD commonly presents with chronic cough
    - → [[Dyspnea]]: COPD commonly presents with exertional breathlessness
    - → [[Expectoration]]: Chronic bronchitis is characterized by sputum production
    - → [[Chest examination]]: COPD warrants a chest examination for breath sounds and hyperinflation
- [[Cough]]
    - → [[Chest examination]]: A cough warrants chest examination for added sounds and consolidation
- [[Dyspnea]]
    - → [[Chest examination]]: Dyspnea warrants a chest examination for percussion, breath sounds and added sounds
- [[Hemoptysis]]
    - → [[Chest examination]]: Hemoptysis warrants chest examination for added sounds and signs of the underlying cause
- [[Obstructive sleep apnea]]
    - → [[Chest examination]]: OSA assessment includes examination of the airway and chest
    - → [[Hypertension]]: OSA is a recognized cause of hypertension
- [[Expectoration]]
- [[Tuberculosis]]
    - → [[Hemoptysis]]: Tuberculosis is an important cause of hemoptysis
    - → [[Cough]]: Tuberculosis commonly presents with chronic cough
    - → [[Abdominal - Constitutional symptoms]]: Tuberculosis is a systemic disease with characteristic constitutional symptoms
    - → [[Chest examination]]: Tuberculosis warrants a chest examination for consolidation and effusion
