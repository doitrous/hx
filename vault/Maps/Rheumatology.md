# Rheumatology

Map of content for the Rheumatology bundles.

## History

- [[Rheum - related symptoms]]
    - → [[Rheum - Joint pain]]: A positive screen opens the joint pain bundle for onset, site and character
    - → [[Rheum - Joint swelling]]: A positive screen opens the joint swelling bundle for site, onset and pattern
    - → [[Rheum - Joint deformity and disability]]: A positive screen opens the deformity and disability bundle
    - → [[Rheum - Extra-articular symptoms]]: Autoimmune rheumatic disease often has multisystem extra-articular involvement
    - → [[Rheum - Joint examination]]: A rheumatological complaint warrants the joint examination for swelling, deformity and range of movement
    - → [[Rheum - Special tests]]: Joint or spinal symptoms warrant the relevant special tests such as Schober or sacroiliac testing
    - → [[Abdominal - Constitutional symptoms]]: A positive screen opens the shared constitutional symptom bundle used across systems for a systemic or malignant cause
- [[Rheum - Extra-articular symptoms]]
    - → [[Cardiac - related symptoms]]: Autoimmune rheumatic disease can cause pericarditis, myocarditis or conduction disease
    - → [[Chest - related symptoms]]: Autoimmune rheumatic disease can cause pleuritis or interstitial lung disease
    - → [[Abdominal - related symptoms]]: Scleroderma and other connective tissue disease involve gut motility and cause dysphagia
    - → [[Neuro - related symptoms]]: Autoimmune rheumatic disease can cause CNS or peripheral nerve vasculitis
    - → [[Urinary symptoms]]: Lupus nephritis and other autoimmune renal disease present with urinary abnormalities
    - → [[Edema - limb swelling]]: Lupus nephritis causes fluid retention and edema
    - → [[Heme - Bleeding tendency]]: Autoimmune rheumatic disease can cause thrombocytopenia and a bleeding tendency
    - → [[Renal - Chronic renal failure]]: Lupus nephritis is a major cause of renal impairment in connective tissue disease
- [[Rheum - Gout]]
    - → [[Rheum - Joint pain]]: A gout flare is analyzed further in the joint pain bundle
    - → [[Renal - Chronic renal failure]]: Chronic kidney disease is both a risk factor for and complication of gout
- [[Rheum - Joint deformity and disability]]
    - → [[Rheum - Joint examination]]: Joint deformity requires the joint examination to characterize it
    - → [[Rheum - Special tests]]: Joint deformity is further assessed with special provocative tests
- [[Rheum - Joint pain]]
    - → [[Rheum - Joint examination]]: Joint pain requires the joint examination for swelling, tenderness and range of movement
    - → [[Rheum - Special tests]]: Joint or spinal pain is further assessed with special provocative tests
- [[Rheum - Joint swelling]]
    - → [[Rheum - Joint examination]]: Joint swelling requires the joint examination for effusion, deformity and range of movement
    - → [[Rheum - Special tests]]: Joint swelling is further assessed with special provocative tests
- [[Rheum - Osteoporosis]]
    - → [[Rheum - Long-term steroid therapy]]: Long-term steroid use is a major cause of secondary osteoporosis
    - → [[Menstrual history]]: Postmenopausal estrogen deficiency is a major risk factor for osteoporosis
- [[Rheum - Rheumatoid arthritis and connective tissue disease]]
    - → [[Rheum - Extra-articular symptoms]]: Connective tissue disease commonly has multisystem extra-articular involvement detailed in that bundle
    - → [[Renal - Chronic renal failure]]: Lupus nephritis is a major cause of renal impairment in SLE
    - → [[Rheum - Long-term steroid therapy]]: Connective tissue disease is commonly treated with long-term steroids detailed in that bundle
- [[Rheum - Long-term steroid therapy]]
    - → [[Rheum - Rheumatoid arthritis and connective tissue disease]]: Long-term steroids are commonly given to treat connective tissue disease
    - → [[Diabetes mellitus]]: Long-term steroid use can cause or worsen diabetes
